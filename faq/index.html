Frequently asked questions

What SEEON is, what it is not, and what happens to your data.

For business travellers

What is SEEON?

SEEON shows you which professionals around you are relevant to what you do, wherever business takes you: at the airport, on the train, in the hotel, in the city in the evening, at a trade fair or a congress. Only people who chose to be visible appear. Whom you approach is up to you.

Does SEEON only work at trade fairs and events?

No. SEEON is built for the whole business trip. Travelling alone, you see relevant people in the lounge or at the hotel bar. In your company or association, you see colleagues or members who happen to be in the same city on the same day. At a trade fair or congress, SEEON can start before the event: if the organiser sets it up, attendees already see each other on the way there.

Is SEEON a matchmaking app?

No. SEEON does not decide whom you should meet, and it does not book meetings for you. It makes visible who is around you and relevant, based on what people offer and what they are looking for. The conversation is yours.

Can anyone see where I am?

No. You enter a White Room, for example in a hotel, a lounge or at an event, with a QR code or a password, and SEEON checks once that you are on site. After that there is no tracking, no map and no live location. Others only see that you are in the same White Room, never where you are standing.

What is a White Room, and who can see me there?

A White Room is a closed space for one place or one group: a trade fair, a hotel, a lounge, a company or an association. Only people in the same White Room can see each other. You decide how visible you are, and you can switch it off at any time.

Do I need a LinkedIn account?

No. At an event you sign in with your badge, and your professional details come from the organiser. In a company or association, the organisation confirms that you belong to it. You can add LinkedIn later if you want to connect there.

What happens after an encounter?

You talk. If you want to stay in touch, you can connect via LinkedIn or continue in the app. Contact details are only shared when both sides want it.

For organisers

Do we need a new app?

No. SEEON runs inside your existing event app or as a standalone app. Attendees do not have to use it. It is always their choice.

What data do we get?

Aggregated, anonymised figures, for example how many attendees activated SEEON, how many contacts were made and in which areas encounters happened. You never see who met whom or who was where.

What happens after closing time?

Encounters do not stop at the hall doors. Hotels near the venue can open their own White Room, so attendees see relevant people in the lobby or at the bar. Attendees can also make themselves visible across the city for the duration of the event, but only if they choose to.

For companies and associations

Can our company see who was where?

No. Employers and associations only get aggregated usage figures, such as activations and contacts made. Never who was where, and never who met whom.

How do you check who belongs to our company or association?

Through your own systems. SEEON only receives the confirmation that someone is a member or employee. Your organisation never sees where its people are.

Status and data

Can I use SEEON today?

SEEON is in development. First deployments with partners are being prepared. If you are an organiser, a company or an association, book a 15-minute call.

What happens to my data?

Nothing is stored permanently, and you do not need a permanent profile. At a trade fair or congress, you sign in with your badge. Your data stays in SEEON for the event, one day before and one day after, and, if the organiser sets it up, during the journey there. It is deleted no later than 72 hours after the event ends. Without an event, your data is kept for 72 hours at most. SEEON is built in Europe and designed for the GDPR.
